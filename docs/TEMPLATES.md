# 🎨 CVGen - Professional Templates Guide

## Overview

CVGen offers 6 professionally designed templates, each optimized for different industries and career levels. All templates are ATS-optimized, follow international design standards, and provide comprehensive add/remove functionality.

---

## 📋 Available Templates

### 1. **Professional Standard** (Default)
**Best for**: General professionals, business roles, entry to mid-level positions

**Features**:
- Classic single-column layout
- Traditional header with contact information
- Standard section ordering
- Maximum ATS compatibility
- Clean, professional appearance

**Section Order**: Summary → Experience → Education → Skills → Certifications → Projects

### 2. **Executive Minimal**
**Best for**: C-suite executives, senior leadership, board members

**Features**:
- Ultra-clean, minimal design
- Centered layout with generous whitespace
- Executive-focused typography
- Leadership achievement emphasis
- Sophisticated, high-end appearance

**Section Order**: Executive Summary → Core Competencies → Executive Experience → Education → Professional Certifications

### 3. **Technical Two-Column**
**Best for**: Software engineers, developers, technical professionals

**Features**:
- Two-column layout with sidebar
- Skills prominently displayed in sidebar
- Certifications in sidebar
- Technical project emphasis
- Code-friendly formatting

**Layout**:
- **Left Sidebar**: Technical Skills, Certifications
- **Right Main**: Summary, Experience, Projects, Education

### 4. **Creative Modern**
**Best for**: Designers, artists, creative professionals, marketers

**Features**:
- Asymmetrical, modern layout
- Visual elements and creative typography
- Portfolio-focused sections
- Creative project emphasis
- Artistic, innovative appearance

**Section Order**: Creative Profile → Professional Experience → Portfolio & Projects → Creative Skills → Education

### 5. **Academic Comprehensive**
**Best for**: Researchers, educators, academics, PhD holders

**Features**:
- Traditional academic CV format
- Research-focused sections
- Comprehensive information display
- Academic achievement emphasis
- Formal, scholarly appearance

**Section Order**: Research Interests → Education → Academic Experience → Research Projects → Technical Skills → Certifications & Licenses

### 6. **European Standard**
**Best for**: European job applications, international positions

**Features**:
- Europass CV format compliance
- European standard structure
- Grid-based contact layout
- Standardized section titles
- International compatibility

**Section Order**: Personal Statement → Work Experience → Education and Training → Skills and Competences → Certificates and Diplomas → Projects

## 🔄 Template Switching

### How It Works
1. **Select Template**: Choose from template selector
2. **Content Preservation**: All your data is preserved
3. **Section Adaptation**: Sections adapt to new template
4. **Customization Reset**: Some settings may reset to template defaults

### What Changes When Switching
- ✅ **Layout**: Visual design and structure
- ✅ **Section Order**: Sections reorder based on template
- ✅ **Section Visibility**: Some sections may show/hide
- ✅ **Typography**: Font sizes and spacing may adjust
- ❌ **Content**: Your text and data remain unchanged
- ❌ **Customizations**: Most settings are preserved

### Best Practices
- **Choose First**: Select template before adding content
- **Test Different Options**: Try multiple templates to find the best fit
- **Industry Match**: Match template to your industry
- **Content Adaptation**: Adjust content to match template focus

## 🚀 Getting Started with Templates

### Step 1: Choose Your Template
1. Open CVGen
2. Click on template selector
3. Browse the 6 available templates
4. Select the template that best fits your industry/career level

### Step 2: Customize Your Content
1. Add your personal information
2. Use add/remove buttons to manage sections
3. Fill in your experience, education, skills, etc.
4. Customize using the sidebar options

### Step 3: Fine-tune Appearance
1. Use Quick Customizer for basic changes
2. Open Advanced Customization for detailed control
3. Adjust sections in the Sections tab
4. Preview and export when ready

### Step 4: Export and Share
1. Download as PDF for applications
2. Export JSON for backup
3. Print for physical copies
4. Share your professional resume
