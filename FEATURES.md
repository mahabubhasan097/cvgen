## 🎨 Customization Panel

Click the **"Customize"** button to access powerful customization options:

### Theme & Colors Tab

#### Preset Themes
Choose from 5 professional color themes:
- **Classic Black** - Traditional, timeless
- **Professional Blue** - Corporate, trustworthy
- **Modern Purple** - Creative, innovative
- **Tech Green** - Fresh, tech-forward
- **Executive Gray** - Sophisticated, elegant

#### Custom Colors
- **Primary Color**: Headers and name color
- **Border Color**: Section dividers and accents
- Use color pickers for unlimited customization

#### Header Styles
Choose how section headers appear:
- **Underline**: Classic bottom border (ATS-friendly)
- **Background**: Filled background for bold look
- **Border**: Full border for structured appearance

---

### Sections Tab

#### Add/Remove Sections
- Toggle visibility of any section with one click
- Hidden sections won't appear in your resume or PDF
- Useful for customizing resume for different jobs

#### Reorder Sections
- Use ⬆️ ⬇️ arrows to move sections up or down
- Change the order to highlight your strengths
- Common orders:
  - Entry Level: Education → Skills → Experience
  - Experienced: Experience → Skills → Education
  - Career Change: Skills → Experience → Education

#### Customize Section Titles
- Edit section titles to match your style
- Examples:
  - "Professional Experience" → "Work History"
  - "Technical Skills" → "Core Competencies"
  - "Certifications" → "Professional Development"

---

### Typography Tab

#### Font Sizes
- **Name Size**: 24-40px (Your name at the top)
- **Heading Size**: 14-24px (Section headers)
- **Body Size**: 10-16px (Main content)
- Real-time preview as you adjust
- Optimized for ATS readability

#### Spacing Controls
- **Section Spacing**: 8-32px between sections
- **Line Spacing**: 2-12px between lines
- Adjust for compact or spacious layouts
- Helps fit content on one page or add breathing room

## 💡 Use Cases & Strategies

### Job Application Optimization
1. **For Backend Roles**:
   - Move "Technical Skills" before "Experience"
   - Hide "Projects" if they're frontend-focused
   - Use "Tech Green" or "Professional Blue" theme

2. **For Leadership Roles**:
   - Move "Experience" to top
   - Increase name and heading font sizes
   - Use "Executive Gray" theme

3. **For Creative Tech Roles**:
   - Show "Projects" section
   - Use "Modern Purple" theme
   - Add portfolio links prominently

### Length Management
- **Too Long?**
  - Reduce font sizes slightly
  - Decrease spacing
  - Hide less relevant sections

- **Too Short?**
  - Increase spacing
  - Show "Projects" or "Certifications"
  - Larger fonts for impact

## 🎯 ATS-Friendly Considerations

All customization options maintain ATS compatibility:

✅ **Safe Customizations**:
- All color themes work with ATS
- Underline header style is most ATS-friendly
- Font sizes 10-16px are optimal
- Single-column layout preserved
- No graphics or images

⚠️ **Best Practices**:
- Stick to preset themes for safest bet
- Use "Underline" header style for maximum compatibility
- Keep body font size at 12-14px
- Maintain reasonable spacing
